## How to read these numbers

* **Published metrics are inflated.** The model card warns that the upstream dataset has train/test contamination (the same source images in both splits), so the scores published there do not measure generalization. The *Published* column above is measured again on Brazilian images that model never saw.
* **The fine-tuned numbers are optimistic too.** The best epoch is selected on the valid split, and all splits come from the same fixed traffic cameras (different vehicles, same scenes). The test split has only 12 images.
* **Scenario.** 92 % of the images are motorcycles seen by a traffic camera. A gain here does not prove a gain for cars at a gate; check `scripts/evaluate.py --model <weights>` on your own images before switching models (`SMART_GATE_MODEL=finetuned`).
